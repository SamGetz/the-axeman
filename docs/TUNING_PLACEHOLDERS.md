# Provisional tuning and candidate-art register

This is the final manifest for values introduced by the playable campaign
through M14. Nothing listed here is silently approved; each remains a labelled
placeholder until Sam signs off a measured play or visual review. Existing M8
Mechanical Splitter values already marked approved in their resources remain
approved and are not reopened by this register.

All global tuning values named below now live as typed, documented sections of
`the-axeman/data/game_config.tres`. Authored content catalogues remain separate.

## Commissions and craftsmanship

- `the-axeman/data/commission_table.tres`: 5% mixed/frontier cash-anchor ratio,
  quick/standard/bulk effort quantities, rotation ratios and generation bands.
- `the-axeman/scenes/2d_management/yard_hud.gd`: aggregate receipt hold/fade and
  per-extra-line timing.
- `the-axeman/data/game_config.tres` — Craftsmanship: rough/clean/exceptional geometry
  thresholds and manual premium multipliers.
- `the-axeman/data/customer_table.tres`: reputation gates, history bound,
  customer premiums and representative order requirements/copy.

## Yard, automation and logistics

- `the-axeman/data/yard_stage_table.tres`: landmark identity, tint, staging and
  authored-state presentation roles. The derived stage logic is structural;
  visual proportions and final art are provisional.
- `the-axeman/data/logistics_upgrade_table.tres`: six purchase costs and order.
  The direct-purchase identities/order are implemented; costs require pacing.
- `the-axeman/data/game_config.tres` — Company Simulation: queue capacities, dispatch
  capacity, cycle time, offline cap, ledger bound and bulk fulfilment quantities.
- `the-axeman/data/hydraulic_split_bank.tres`: machine cost and added capacity.
- `the-axeman/data/upgrade_table.tres`: all sixteen M15 reinvestment rows—their
  lifetime-earnings thresholds, fixed base costs/growth, rank caps, interval
  floors and production magnitudes. These must retain the exact four-hour
  placeholder label until deterministic cautious/expected/optimized campaigns
  and the real-time fresh-save review are complete.
- The current maximum planetary candidate (including 220,000 trees per Fleet
  rank) is a backward projection that reaches zero inside a 16.5–17 minute final
  production band;
  it is not approved tuning and must not compensate for unresolved early bands.

## Regions, routes and continental company

- `the-axeman/data/regional_network_table.tres`: region reputation/standing
  gates, depot/route/project costs, route times/capacities and processed-output
  thresholds. Species-to-region identity is authored; all numeric pacing is
  provisional.
- `the-axeman/data/company_doctrine_table.tres`: Craft House, Logistics Company
  and Global Specialist effect magnitudes. Doctrine switching is intentionally
  free; magnitudes require comparative play.
- The Supplier Atlas delay copy, regional customer assignment and signature-log
  presentation remain copy/pacing candidates.

## Global mastery and Earth finale

- World Catalogue Archive, Heavy Freight Grid and Global Buyer Exchange costs
  and source-log-equivalent requirements in `regional_network_table.tres`.
- Manual/automated large-number source-log equivalents and milestone headline
  spacing. The historical trillion-scale target is not locked.
- Lignum Vitae three-stage finale count, finale customer copy and celebration
  duration. The manual-only ownership boundary is structural.

## Launch programme

- `the-axeman/data/launch_program_table.tres`: all four project cash/output/
  mastery/contribution requirements and all range/cargo/shielding capabilities.
- `the-axeman/data/expedition_table.tres`: range/shielding gates and 120/240/360
  second flight durations. Flight-time non-acceleration is structural.
- Frontier skill values are finalized in `skill_tree.tres`; launch project
  costs, requirements and non-skill campaign pacing remain provisional.
- Mission Control, gantry and vessel proportions, signage placement and yard
  presentation timing.

## Alien campaign and company

- `the-axeman/data/alien_wood_table.tres`: split odds, XP, manual mastery targets,
  fragment caps, scar counts, gravity scales, fleet/orbital costs and premium
  multipliers for all three alien woods.
- `the-axeman/data/game_config.tres` — Alien Company: fleet cap, cargo interval, logs per
  fleet, orbital-line output and receipt cap.
- Alien profile/certification copy, quarantine/identification pacing, repeat
  cargo messaging and charter presentation.
- The behavior contracts are structural and bounded: Spiralwood offers one next
  aligned assist, Tideglass never exceeds its fragment cap, and Cinderheart
  consumes one non-recursive scar assist.

## Proc-driven shop equipment

- `the-axeman/data/equipment_upgrade_table.tres`: all sixteen axe/stump prices,
  reliability/radius steps, independent proc chances and auxiliary rank prices,
  growth and passive magnitudes. The player-approved identities, eight-stage
  chains, gates and roles are structural; every number remains provisional.
- `mastery_echo`'s 20-event and `express_handoff`'s 12-event dry-streak bounds
  in `proc_table.tres` are provisional. Existing Double Chop, Follow-Up, Quick
  Study and Grain Reader skill chances remain final and were not retuned.
- `equipment_table.tres` tint colours are replaceable visual placeholders. Each
  identity retains its own stable data row and presentation path so final models
  can replace one stage without changing purchase or proc state.

## Replaceable production-candidate art

The detailed prompts, source mode and correction history live in
`the-axeman/assets/generated/MANIFEST.md`. The current candidates are:

- Supplier Atlas background.
- Instrument-maker customer portrait.
- Earth Master headline panel.
- Mission Control emblem.
- Resonant Spiralwood, Tideglass Timber and Cinderheart Timber surface textures.

Each candidate was generated separately, inspected at full size, corrected once
with a targeted edit, integrated under a stable descriptive filename and
rendered in the Compatibility pipeline. Alien fresh/scarred/cut 1280×720
captures show distinct materials, tiling at gameplay scale and readable cut
faces. These assets retain AI provenance and replaceable status; native machine,
vehicle, headquarters and space geometry also remains eventual final-art work.
